A Story of Devotion

The Race Around the World

Shiva challenged his sons to circle the world three times. Murugan sped off on his peacock — but Ganesha simply walked around his parents, for to him, they were the whole world.

Ganesha — one calm circle Murugan — racing the world

Both paths end the same way: wisdom isn't measured in distance.
